## 7. Consequences for later phases
* **Markers in FPDB are rare** (9/300 = 3.0% [1.6%, 5.6%] of audited images) and not enriched in standard planes, so the
  'calipers ⇒ standard plane' shortcut cannot be a major driver *within* FPDB. E5a (marker inpainting) has 0 detector∧audit-positive test images → **E5a infeasible**; E5d dropped (no pairs).
* **On-screen text/UI is universal in FPDB** (100% of audited images) and the UI detector is accurate (test recall 0.97, precision 1.00). The overlay axis that remains testable is therefore **UI/out-of-sector content** (E4 non-anatomical / overlay-only models, E5 `a_text` and `b_*` perturbations), not calipers.
* **External sets differ in the opposite direction:** calipers/measurement graphics are frequent in Ghana, Uganda and UCL but rare in FPDB training, and the African images carry little or no UI. This is a train→test overlay shift to report in R2/R3. Synthetic caliper augmentation (A2) keeps its rationale for R5; test-time marker inpainting is limited by detector recall (it works for Ghana's '+' markers, detector rate ≈0.97).
* **Exact duplicates** (see results/json/R1_exact_duplicates.json): byte-identical FPDB images exist within patients (never across splits); 8 test-split groups carry contradictory labels for the identical image. Splits stay frozen; patient-clustered CIs (R2) account for the within-patient redundancy.
